How it works

Four steps from graduate to job-ready

We do not sell courses. We show you which skills to learn first, then point you to free or low-cost resources for each one.

1

Tell us about you

Your degree, institution, graduation year, interests and the roles you want.

2

See your gaps

Missing skills ranked by how much employers ask for them.

3

Follow your roadmap

A step-by-step plan with free or low-cost resources linked to every step.

4

Track your progress

Tick off steps and watch your readiness score rise.

About us

Built for graduates who are ready to work

Many African graduates finish university without knowing which skills employers want. Skill Gap Analyzer is Phase 1 of a larger mission: career mapping and upskilling pathways for African youth.

The project is built by Ineza Henry Jayz, a Software Engineering student at African Leadership University in Kigali, Rwanda.
